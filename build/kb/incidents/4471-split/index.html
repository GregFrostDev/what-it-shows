#4471 is three problems, not one

owner support desk proposed reviewed 2026-11-10 · 0 days ago

#4471 says “one issue, one fix queued”. The desk’s own code and notes say otherwise: the reports merged into it come from three different problems, and the queued parser fix covers only the first.

Shipping the parser fix will not close #4471. Reports from booking-v3 forms and the one-day moves will keep coming.

The three problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads dates month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. Day and month swap: “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.”
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. Fixing the parser does not touch this.
  • one-day: the date moves by one day, not by a swap. “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday”, and one meeting landed on Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto. That points to time zones. The desk already listed these reports in desk-notes.json; the #4471 page still files them under parser with a footnote.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on the subject line, and these reports share a handful of subjects. A subject says what the reporter saw, not what broke. Intake tags follow the reporter’s browser, not the tenant, so they disagree with the cause too.

What to do

  • Keep #4471 for parser, and track hint and one-day as their own tickets.
  • When a big ticket gets a fix queued, check whether every report in it would be closed by that fix.
